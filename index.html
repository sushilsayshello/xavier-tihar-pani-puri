Terms & Conditions

Official competition guidelines for the Xavier Academy Tihar Festival Lucky Draw Campaign.

1. Organizer & Event Context

This promotional lucky draw is organized by the Xavier Academy Tihar Festival Pani Puri Stall Committee as an engagement activity during the campus Tihar celebration.

2. Eligibility Criteria

  • Participation is open to enrolled Xavier Academy students (Grade 11, Grade 12) and authorized campus guests.
  • Participants under the age of 18 must participate with the acknowledgment and consent of a parent or legal guardian.

3. Free Entry (No Purchase Necessary)

No food purchase or payment at the pani puri stall is required to participate in the lucky draw. Eligible participants enter by submitting their responses through the survey form.

4. Entry Limits & Fraud Prevention

Strictly one entry per student. Duplicate submissions using identical phone numbers or automated scripts will be excluded from the final drawing list.

5. Official Prize Specification

The designated prize is one (1) T900 Ultra Smartwatch (49mm casing, 2.09-inch screen, wireless magnetic charger), reference market retail value NPR 689 (as cataloged on Daraz Nepal). No cash alternative or prize exchange will be offered.

6. Winner Selection & Announcement Schedule

  • Draw Schedule: The draw will be conducted post-festival over the weekend using random digital selection across the collected spreadsheet registry.
  • Contact Method: The drawn winner will be directly reached via the mobile number and email provided during entry.
  • Verification: The winner must present a valid student identification card (or proof of identity) prior to prize collection.

7. Data Privacy & Usage

All information provided in the survey is stored in a private Google Sheet. Personal data is utilized exclusively for prize administration, eligibility verification, and promotional communications as consented to by the participant.
