📜 Terms & Conditions

  1. Organiser: The event organiser must be identified by full legal or responsible organisation name before launch.
  2. Eligibility: Xavier Academy Year 11 and Year 12 students may enter, subject to the school's approval and these rules.
  3. No purchase: No food purchase or payment is required to enter.
  4. Prize: One smartwatch. The organiser should state the exact make and model before entries open.
  5. Entry limit: One entry per student. Duplicate, incomplete or fraudulent entries may be disqualified.
  6. Winner selection: One winner will be selected randomly at the end of the festival. The draw should be documented by the organiser.
  7. Winner verification: The winner may be asked to verify their identity and student eligibility.
  8. Prize collection: The winner must follow the organiser's collection instructions. A parent or guardian may need to assist a minor.
  9. Under-18 participants: Parent or guardian approval is required before entering and submitting personal information.
  10. Privacy: Information is collected for entry administration, eligibility checks and winner contact. The organiser must specify who controls the information, how to request access or correction, how to contact the organiser and when information will be deleted.
  11. Marketing: Marketing permission is separate and optional. Declining it does not affect entry or the chance of winning. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.
  12. Changes: Material changes or cancellation will be communicated by the organiser where necessary.

Privacy and data handling

Survey details must only be accessible to authorised organisers. They must not be displayed publicly or shared with unrelated organisations. Marketing communications should only be sent where valid consent has been obtained.

The organiser should obtain school approval and review the applicable privacy, minor-consent and promotional competition requirements before the event.
