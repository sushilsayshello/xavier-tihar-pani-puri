Terms, Rules & Privacy Notice

Guidelines for the Xavier Academy Tihar Festival Pani Puri Stall, WhatsApp Ordering & T900 Ultra Smartwatch Lucky Draw.

🪔 Summary for Participants: Entry into the lucky draw is 100% free with no food purchase necessary. All student details are kept in a private, encrypted Google Sheet and handled with strict confidentiality.

1. Stall Organizers & Context

The Pani Puri Stall, WhatsApp ordering service, and student survey are operated by the Xavier Academy Tihar Festival Organizing Committee as part of the student festival celebration in Kathmandu, Nepal.

2. Eligibility Criteria

  • Open Participants: Currently enrolled students of Xavier Academy (Grade 11 and Grade 12), faculty, alumni, and invited campus guests.
  • Under-18 Participants: Participants under the age of 18 must participate with the acknowledgment and consent of a parent or legal guardian. Submitting the survey confirms that such permission has been granted.

3. Free Entry & "No Purchase Necessary"

No purchase, donation, or stall order is required to enter or win the lucky draw. Purchasing pani puri does not increase or alter the odds of winning. Entry is earned solely by completing the student survey honestly on lucky-draw.html.

4. Entry Limit & Anti-Fraud Controls

  • Strictly one (1) entry per student.
  • Submissions with duplicate mobile numbers, duplicate emails, automated script attacks, or intentionally fabricated student details will be invalidated prior to the draw.
  • The committee reserves the right to disqualify entries that disrupt the integrity of the festival event.

5. Featured Prize Specifications

🎁 Designated Competition Prize:
  • Item: One (1) T900 Ultra Smartwatch (49mm casing)
  • Features: 2.09-inch HD touch screen, dual working buttons, wireless magnetic charger dock
  • Reference Market Value: NPR 689 (Verified catalog price on Daraz Nepal)

The prize is non-transferable, and no cash equivalents or item exchanges will be substituted.

6. Random Drawing & Winner Notification

  • Draw Schedule: The draw will be conducted over the weekend immediately following the festival.
  • Selection Process: The winner will be chosen at random using an automated digital script across all valid entries in the private Google Sheet registry.
  • Notification: The selected winner will be contacted directly via WhatsApp/phone call and their submitted email address.
  • Identity Verification: The winner must present a valid Xavier Academy student ID or recognized proof of identity to claim their smartwatch. Minors may be asked to receive their prize alongside a guardian.
  • Unclaimed Prizes: If a winner cannot be reached or fails to verify eligibility within 5 calendar days, a secondary random draw will be conducted.

7. Food Ordering & WhatsApp Checkout Terms

  • All food orders placed on index.html generate a unique ticket code (e.g., XAT-XXXX-XXX).
  • Manual Verification: Orders are verified manually by stall volunteers when the customer shares their Kumari Bank QR payment screenshot via WhatsApp. The website does not execute automated banking clearance.
  • Pickup: Customers must present their ticket number and confirmed payment at the stall counter for food collection.

8. Privacy Notice & Data Protection

We respect your privacy and handle student information responsibly:

  • What We Collect: Full name, WhatsApp/phone number, email, academic grade, study interests, and stall feedback.
  • Storage & Access: Data is transmitted directly to a private, access-restricted Google Sheet via Google Apps Script. No data is stored in publicly accessible client-side scripts or public CSV downloads.
  • Purpose of Use: Information is used exclusively to:
    1. Validate lucky draw entries and contact the prize winner.
    2. Analyze aggregate student preference data for campus academic and festival planning.
    3. Send opted-in event updates or educational notifications (which can be opted out of at any time).
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Student records will never be rented, sold, or shared with unauthorized commercial advertisers.
